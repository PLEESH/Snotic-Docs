Correction and third-party rights
=================================
The original WP-CLI notice inventory omitted psr/container 1.0.0 and
wp-cli/wp-cli-tests v4.3.14 even though their files are shipped in the PHAR.
The psr/container copyright notices were absent. The WP-CLI tests MIT notice
text appears in other supplied WP-CLI notices, but its inventory entry was
omitted. The exact upstream notices for both components are reproduced below.
The full member review also identifies nested Requests 2.0.12, its ComplexPie
IRI parser, and Mozilla CA certificate data within wp-cli/wp-cli. Their distinct
notices and licence terms are included here; the container's MIT notice alone
does not cover these nested components. Bootstrap attribution is corrected
to the wp-cli/wp-cli-bundle project, whose unchanged notice is already shipped.
Upstream licence rights remain unaffected by the core Snotic use terms.
This addendum grants no additional rights in the proprietary core application.
